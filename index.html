How it works

The forecast

National Energy System Operator's Carbon Intensity API publishes grams of CO2 per kWh for every half-hour of the next 48 hours, for Great Britain, its three nations and 14 regions, with the generation mix behind it. Grid Tide fetches it straight from your browser. No account, no tracking; your settings stay in your browser.

The pick

For a machine that runs for n half-hours, every possible start between now and your deadline is scored by its average carbon over the whole run. The cleanest wins; ties go to the earliest start so you wait no longer than needed. CO2 per run is energy times that average.

What it misses

The forecast is the grid's average intensity. What a shifted load truly avoids depends on the power station that ramps up or down for it, usually gas at peak and, on windy nights, wind that would otherwise be curtailed, so treat the grams as an estimate. Live data covers Great Britain only. Machine figures are typical values; type in the ones on your energy label.
